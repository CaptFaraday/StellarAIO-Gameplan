## Risk Factors

### Order Cancellations
- Retailers can cancel orders detected as bot purchases
- Using proper proxies and profile diversification reduces this risk

### Market Saturation
- As more users adopt bots, competition increases
- Early movers on drops see highest success rates

### Retailer Countermeasures
- Sites continuously update anti-bot protection
- Stellar's success depends on developers keeping up with changes
- Some users report sub-5% success rates during heavy competition

### Resale Platform Fees
- eBay: ~13% final value fee
- StockX: 8-9.5% transaction fee
- TCGplayer: 10.25-12.55% + payment processing
- Always factor fees into profit calculations

### Print Run Risks
- High-demand sets may receive additional print runs
- Increased supply reduces resale premiums
- Pokemon 151 and Prismatic Evolutions are confirmed for additional prints

## Key Takeaways

1. **Profit is real but variable** - Success depends on setup quality, timing, and market conditions

2. **Volume matters** - Users running higher task counts with proper infrastructure see significantly better returns

3. **Pokemon Center is king** - PC exclusive products consistently command the highest premiums

4. **Hot sets = guaranteed profit** - Prismatic Evolutions, Pokemon 151, and Destined Rivals at retail are essentially guaranteed profit

5. **ROI timeline** - Most active users report recovering their license cost within 1-3 successful drops

6. **Infrastructure investment** - Proper proxy setup (80% residential, 20% ISP for Pokemon Center) dramatically improves success rates
