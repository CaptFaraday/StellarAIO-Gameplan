## Maximizing Profit: Core Strategies

### Strategy 1: Follow the Release Tiers

Divine typically categorizes releases by profit potential:

| Tier | Characteristics | Strategy |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| **S-Tier** | High hype, massive profit | Full resource deployment, max tasks |
| **A-Tier** | Solid profit, medium competition | Strong effort, 50-75% resources |
| **B-Tier** | Moderate profit, easier | Casual effort, good for practice |
| **C-Tier** | Break-even or slight profit | Skip unless personal want |
| **Avoid** | Negative resale value | Do not cop |

**Focus your energy on S and A tier releases**. Spreading thin across every drop wastes resources and leads to burnout.

### Strategy 2: Use the Profit Calculator

Before every release, calculate your break-even:

```
Profit = Resale Price - Retail Price - Fees - Shipping - Operating Costs

Example:
Resale: $350
Retail: $180
StockX Fee (10%): -$35
Shipping: -$15
Proxies (per drop): -$10
---
Net Profit: $110

Minimum pairs needed for break-even: 1
Target pairs for good profit: 3-5
```

### Strategy 3: Multi-Site Coverage

Divine monitors alert on multiple retailers. Maximize coverage by:

1. **Pre-create tasks** for all sites carrying the product
2. **Use site-appropriate proxies** (see Stella Proxies guide)
3. **Stagger task starts** to avoid rate limits
4. **Don't ignore sleeper sites** - less competition on regional retailers

### Strategy 4: Quick-Flip vs Hold Strategy

| Approach | When to Use | Pros | Cons |
|----------|-------------|------|------|
| **Quick Flip** | Need cash flow, uncertain market | Fast money, reduced risk | Lower peak profit |
| **Hold** | Strong market indicators, limited supply | Maximum profit potential | Capital tied up, storage |

Divine's market analysis helps identify which strategy fits each product.

### Strategy 5: Diversification

Don't put all eggs in one basket:

- **Product Mix**: Sneakers, consoles, collectibles
- **Retail Mix**: Shopify, Footsites, general retail
- **Price Points**: High-ticket items and volume plays
- **Timeline**: Quick flips and long-term holds

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Ignoring Release Guides

**Problem**: Using generic settings for every drop
**Solution**: Read Divine's specific guide for each release. Settings that work on Shopify fail on Footsites.

### Mistake 2: Over-Tasking

**Problem**: Running 100 tasks with 25 proxies
**Solution**: Match task count to proxy count. More tasks doesn't mean more success if proxies get burned.

### Mistake 3: Chasing Every Drop

**Problem**: Exhausting resources on low-profit releases
**Solution**: Focus on S and A tier drops. Quality over quantity.

### Mistake 4: Not Using Group Buys

**Problem**: Paying retail for proxies and services
**Solution**: Check Divine's group buy channel. Discounts add up significantly over time.

### Mistake 5: Poor Flip Timing

**Problem**: Selling at the wrong time
**Solution**: Follow Divine's market analysis. Prices often dip right after release, then rise.

### Mistake 6: Single Point of Failure

**Problem**: Relying on one site, one proxy type, one approach
**Solution**: Diversify across sites and proxy types as Divine recommends.

### Mistake 7: Ignoring Community Feedback

**Problem**: Not learning from others' experiences
**Solution**: Read success/failure reports. Avoid their mistakes, copy their wins.
